Fountain solution, also called dampening solution, fount or fountain etch, is the water-based mixture that dampens the lithographic plate before it contacts the inking rollers, keeping non-image areas free of ink. It is supplied as a concentrate and diluted with water at the press.

Because water quality directly affects dampening performance, JNT Suppliers offers separate fountain solution grades for hard and soft water, and an [alcohol-free fountain solution](#jntf-3) for printers reducing or removing [isopropyl alcohol](../isopropyl-alcohol/). Our [plate gum](../plate-gum/) can also be added to the working solution to stop scumming.

Fountain solution is a water-based mixture that dampens the lithographic plate before it contacts the inking rollers, keeping non-image areas free of ink. Supplied as a concentrate (also known as fountain etch), it is diluted with water at the press to produce the dampening solution.

Because water quality directly affects dampening performance, JNT Suppliers offers separate formulations for hard and soft water.

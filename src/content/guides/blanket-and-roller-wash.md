Every offset press needs regular wash-ups: at colour changes, at the end of a run and before the press stands overnight. The wash used affects how quickly the press is ready again, how clean the rollers and blankets stay, and the conditions in the pressroom.

## Blankets and rollers: what is being cleaned

- **The blanket** is the rubber-covered cylinder that takes the inked image from the plate and transfers it to the paper. Ink, paper dust and coating build up on its surface during a run.
- **The rollers** carry ink from the ink duct to the plate, and water from the dampening system. Ink left on them dries, hardens and makes colour changes slower.

Both need a wash that removes ink effectively without harming the rubber and roller materials.

## What water-miscible means

A water-miscible wash mixes with water. On an offset press, the surfaces being cleaned carry both ink and water-based residue from dampening and paper. A wash that mixes with water can lift both in one wash-up, instead of leaving a water-based film behind.

Our [blanket and roller wash](../../products/roller-wash/) is water-miscible, for daily blanket and roller wash-ups, colour changes and presses using water-based clean-up.

## How to use it

Apply the wash to the blanket or rollers with a clean cloth or through the press wash-up system, then wipe or run off. Dilution and wash-up settings depend on the press. Our technical team can give guidance specific to your press.

Good wash-up habits:

- wash up at colour changes and at the end of each run, before ink dries on the rollers
- use clean cloths, so dirt is removed rather than spread around
- check blankets regularly for build-up, glazing or damage

## Safety in the pressroom

Press washes need to be handled with care. Our blanket and roller wash is a flammable liquid:

- keep it away from heat and open flames
- use it with good ventilation
- wear gloves and eye protection
- read the safety data sheet, which we supply with the product

In choosing raw materials, we balance cleaning efficiency with operator safety, environmental impact and material compatibility, and aim to reduce and replace harmful substances wherever an equally effective alternative exists.

## Cleaning plates

Plates need their own cleaner. Washes for blankets and rollers are not designed to remove scum, scratches or oxidation from plates. For that, use a [plate cleaner](../../products/plate-cleaner/) matched to the plate's condition and the inks being run.
